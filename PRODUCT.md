# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences, one anxiety:

1. **Bangladeshi diaspora (NRBs)** — sons and daughters in Toronto, London, Dubai, New York deciding at 11pm who will take their mother to her cardiologist in Dhaka tomorrow. They find the site on a phone, judge trustworthiness in seconds, and convert via WhatsApp. English-first readers, moved by Bengali touches.
2. **Middle-class families in Dhaka** — busy adults who can't leave work to escort an elderly parent to the hospital, collect reports, or receive a relative at the airport or Sadarghat launch terminal. Often browsing on mid-range Android over patchy data.

The job to be done: *"Let me hand over the care of someone I love, to a stranger, without fear."* Every design decision serves that hand-over of trust.

## Product Purpose

Guide is a pre-launch medical companion service in Dhaka. Primary service: Hospital Companion (home → hospital → consultation notes → medicines → reports → safe return, with digital reports sent to family abroad). Secondary: hospital attendant, report/medicine collection, airport & launch terminal escorts, intercity companion. Booking is WhatsApp/phone only — no online payment. The website's success = a worried family member messages on WhatsApp.

## Brand Personality

**Steadfast · Warm · Precise.** The voice of a trusted family aide: calm, direct, never salesy. Bengali is part of the brand's warmth (English primary, Bengali as meaningful accent — never decoration). Emotional goal: the relief of "someone reliable is handling it."

## Anti-references

- **The 2024 v0/AI-template look** (the founder's explicit complaint, twice): generic gradient heroes, glassmorphism, identical icon-card grids, uppercase tracked eyebrows over every section, fake metrics.
- **Fake social proof.** The business is pre-launch: no testimonials, no "500+ clients", no stock photos of non-Bengali people posing as staff. Honesty is a design feature.
- **Western eldercare gloss** (Honor/Papa-style soft-focus stock photography) — reads false here; there are no real photos yet.
- **NGO/charity aesthetics** — Guide is a paid professional service, not aid.

## Design Principles

1. **Trust is built with specifics.** Real terms (50% advance, 3–4h booking window, live tracking) shown plainly beat vague promises. Transparency sections are first-class content, not fine print.
2. **The journey is the brand.** Guide's entire service is accompanying someone along a route (home → hospital → home). The visual system draws that route rather than describing it.
3. **Honest by construction.** Nothing on the page claims what the business can't prove. Illustration and typography over fake photography; process over testimonials.
4. **Two scripts, one voice.** Bengali and English are typeset with equal craft; Bengali carries emotional weight (taglines, reassurance), English carries information.
5. **Works on a worried phone.** Fast on mid-range Android, readable in Dhaka daylight, one thumb to WhatsApp from anywhere on the page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA minimum: body text ≥4.5:1, focus-visible styles everywhere, skip link.
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation.
- Audience includes elderly users and stressed users: generous tap targets (≥44px), no time-limited UI, plain language.
- Bengali text is real text (never images), tagged with `lang="bn"`.
